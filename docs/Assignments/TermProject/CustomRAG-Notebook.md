# Project: A custom AI Notebook

Each of you will build a custom  Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) at notebook.google.com, for a specific purpose. It can relate to the career area you're interested in, your team's focus area, or both. Your notebook's knowledge comes from the sources you add to your notebook: documents you upload (PDFs, Google Docs, slides and so on), web pages, and YouTube videos.

## Make a Custom Gemini Notebook

**Make it better than plain Gemini.** Plain Gemini answers from its general training and the open web. A notebook answers only from your sources and cites them. So the quality of your notebook depends on the sources you choose. Pick a fairly narrow, niche purpose. Then pick sources for their accuracy and relevance, and for information that's hard to find with a general web search. Aim for at least 8 good sources. Ten well-chosen ones beat fifty random ones.

**Your notebook must include:**

1. **Curated sources.** Use a mix of uploaded documents and web or YouTube sources, all chosen with care. Delete any source that's off-topic or unreliable.
2. **Custom chat settings.** In the Chat panel, open *Configure Chat*, choose *Custom*, and write instructions that give your chatbot a role, an audience, and a style. For example: "You are an advisor for first-year welding students. Answer in plain language and point to the relevant safety standard." Set a response length that fits its purpose.
3. **At least two Studio outputs** that suit your purpose. Options include an Audio Overview, a Video Overview, a mind map, a report or study guide, flashcards, a quiz, or an infographic. Pick the ones a real user of your notebook would find useful.
4. **Testing.** Ask your notebook at least five realistic questions. Check that the answers are accurate and that the citations point to the right sources. Revise your sources or instructions if needed.

**Brainstorm in the forum.** Discuss possible notebooks with your team. Each team member should choose a different purpose. Before you submit, share your notebook link in the forum to get feedback from your teammates.

**To share your notebook:** select *Share*, set Notebook Access to *Anyone with a link*, and copy the link. If you only want people to see the chat, use *Copy link to chat view*. Open your link in a private or incognito window to make sure it works.

**In the forum, post:**

- A short description of your chatbot: its purpose and intended audience
- A list of its sources, with a sentence on why you chose them
- The custom instructions you wrote
- Which Studio outputs you made and why
- The share link to your notebook
