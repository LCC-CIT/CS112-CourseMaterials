Here's what you need to know to get started:

### What this course is about

This course provides a non-technical overview of Artificial Intelligence—no prior coding or computer science experience is needed. We’ll explore what AI is, how machine learning and neural networks work, and dive into practical applications of generative AI like prompt engineering and building custom chatbots (**Gemini Gems**). We’ll also examine the ethical issues, societal impacts, and risks and benefits of AI.
